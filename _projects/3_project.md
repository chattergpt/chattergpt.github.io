As part of team Chi-Squad in the American Statistical Association's Datafest hosted by Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), we analyzed comprehensive datasets from Savills’ commercial real estate firm to derive key insights and identify actionable insights for their advisory services. 

We performed exploratory data analysis & feature engineering, alongside linear regression of different numerical & categorical variables such as space availability, unemployment rate, and total revenue & leasing space, and developed a prediction model to determine future direction of rent prices in key industries.

We presented our findings to industry judges & received a Gold Medal Award in the Best Visualization Category.
