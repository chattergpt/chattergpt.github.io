As part of the 2024 Biomedical Data Science Hackathon, I led a team of 4 as team captain to identify associations between chromatin accessibility and gene expression data from immune cells. 

We analyzed data from 2,999 CD14+ monocyte cells, comprising 36,601 genes and 108,344 chromatin accessibility peaks, and extracted biologically relevant features, including mean RNA and ATAC-seq counts and RNA–ATAC correlation. We also trained an **XGBoost classifier** to predict associations in the test dataset.

We achieved an MCC score of 0.9868, placing third overall and first amongst all undergraduate teams.
