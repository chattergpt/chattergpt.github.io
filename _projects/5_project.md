As part of the 2025 Biomedical Data Science Hackathon, I led a team of 4 as team captain to model gene expression responses to double perturbations using machine learning.

We developed a predictive pipeline to estimate genome-wide expression profiles for combinatorial gene knockdowns based on single-gene perturbation data. Our approach involved biologically inspired feature engineering, including additive, interaction, and difference terms, and per-gene LightGBM regressors to capture both linear and nonlinear interaction effects.

We achieved an RMSD score of 0.14234, placing first overall among all competing teams.
